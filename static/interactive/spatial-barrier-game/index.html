SYSTEM / Move a pawn or place a barrier. Inspect locations. Compare geometric and walkable distances on one shared field.

iHOW TO PLAY

A starts south and races north. B starts north and races south. Each turn, move one orthogonal cell or place a two-edge barrier.

Each player has 10 barriers. No overlaps, crossings, jumps or shared cells. Both players must retain a route to their goal.

Switch modes to inspect locations or distances without spending a turn. Distance queries treat pawns as permeable markers.
