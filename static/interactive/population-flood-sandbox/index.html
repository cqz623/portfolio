Population × Flood Sandbox

Manhattan + Brooklyn · paired year scenarios · not a forecast

description

research question

How do the precomputed population and flood-extent scenarios for 2020, 2030, 2040, and 2050 compare in estimated population exposure across Manhattan and Brooklyn?

methodology

A residential parcel is classified as potentially flood-affected when any portion intersects a flooded raster cell. Its full residential-unit-based population allocation is assigned once to a fixed 250 m grid cell using an interior representative point. Parcel-intersection metrics and flood boundaries come from precomputed project files; the counterfactual panel retains the earlier point-based analysis as a historical result.

data sources

  • U.S. Census Bureau — 2020 Decennial Census population
  • NYC Department of City Planning — 2020 Census Tracts
  • NYC Department of City Planning — Population Projections for 2030–2055
  • NYC Department of City Planning — MapPLUTO
  • U.S. Geological Survey — 3DEP Digital Elevation Model

limitations

These are simplified modeled scenarios, not validated hydrodynamic forecasts or observed outcomes. Water levels represent simplified high tide plus sea-level rise, not storm surge. Future population keeps the 2020 spatial distribution within each borough; residential-unit allocation is a proxy, not actual occupancy.

Flood defenses, building-floor elevations, daytime movement, evacuation, redevelopment, and detailed hydrodynamics are not represented.
